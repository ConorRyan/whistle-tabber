The fingering editor opens any stored tune, keeping repeats, ornaments
and bar lines (a tune it must convert opens as a copy, with a notice).
Tapping the rhythm detects triplets. Every note that has more than one
fingering gets its choice in Settings. The library exports the selected
tunes from the selection bar, and pasted sets without X: lines split
into their tunes. The tune screen scrolls its title away with the music
and plays from one row in landscape.
