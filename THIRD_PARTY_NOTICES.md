## Bundled tunes

| Data | Location | License |
|---|---|---|
| O'Neill's Music of Ireland (1850) ABC transcriptions, [O'Neill's Project](https://trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/book/ONeills/_1850/) | `app/src/main/assets/tunes/oneills.abc` (the starter set); test fixtures in `app/src/androidTest/assets/tunes/` and `web-tests/fixtures/` | GNU GPL version 2 or later |

The transcriptions are © 1997–2000 the contributors to the O'Neill's Project.
Each tune keeps its transcriber credit in its `Z:` field.

The starter set `oneills.abc` holds 50 tunes listed in
`scripts/starter-tunes.txt` and is generated by `scripts/build-starter-set.sh`.
Our changes: each tune's `R:` line is set from the list, so the library's
rhythm filter works. One tune with no `Z:` line (No. 1700, from Nick
Terhorst's file) gets a credit line saying where it came from. Trailing spaces
are removed. The music is unchanged.

## Fingering data

Third-octave tin whistle fingerings follow the chart in the
[Woodwind Fingering Guide](https://www.wfg.woodwind.org/tinwhistle/tw_bas_3.html)
(© 1998–2005 Timothy Reichard). Fingerings are facts; the source is credited
in `FingeringEngine.kt`. The other fingerings are standard tin whistle
technique, written for this project.

## Sounds

Playback samples are synthesised at runtime by the app's own code
(`WhistleSynth`); no soundfont is bundled.
