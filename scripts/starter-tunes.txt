# Starter set: ten of the best-known Irish session tunes, as printed in
# O'Neill's Music of Ireland (1850), O'Neill's Project transcriptions (GPL-2+),
# fetched by scripts/build-starter-set.sh.
#
# Each fits a D whistle without octave folding (written D to b'), has no
# accidentals beyond C natural, and parses in abcjs without warnings.
#
# Format: <file in _1850/X/> | <R: value we set> | <title, for humans>
#         [| <Z: credit to add when the file has no Z: line>]
# Many 1850 transcriptions have no R: line, and the rest spell rhythms
# differently ("slipjig", "double jig"), so the script sets R: from column 2.

# Reels
1425_bs.abc | reel | Drowsy Maggie
1509_jw.abc | reel | The Merry Blacksmith
1518_jw.abc | reel | The Wind that Shakes the Barley

# Jigs
0776_sf.abc | jig | Banish Misfortune
0795_sf.abc | jig | Out on the Ocean
0987_rt.abc | jig | Haste to the Wedding

# Hornpipes
1603_mh.abc | hornpipe | The Harvest Home
1628_nt.abc | hornpipe | Off to California
1700_nt.abc | hornpipe | The Boys of Bluehill | Transcribed by Nick Terhorst (O'Neill's Project file F/1625-1700_nt.abc; credit line added by Whistle Tabber)

# March
1814_bs.abc | march | The Rakes of Mallow
